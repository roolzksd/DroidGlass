Bendthelight

        
Liquid Glass pulls the backdrop toward the centre at the rim, like a thick lens. A hairline highlight runs along the edge and a soft rainbow shows in the corners. Plain glassmorphism only blurs the background under a white haze, which is why it looks flat.

      

        
Glass on a white page

        
On white there is nothing to bend, so transparent glass almost disappears. The engine darkens the inside of the rim slightly, like thick glass, and adds a soft shadow: that is what separates the control from the page.

        

        
Apple's rule of thumb: glass belongs to the control layer that floats above content. Lists, cards and the page background stay solid. Use it for a tab bar, a floating button, a toolbar, a sheet.

        
Text on glass needs contrast. Over a busy backdrop add blur and a light surface, or a veil in the page colour under the labels, as the tab bar below does.

        

        
Everything moves on springs with Compose parameters: press growth, the jelly of a pressed thumb, the rubber band of a pulled button and its bounce back.
